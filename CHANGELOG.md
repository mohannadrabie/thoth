**Deleted** `NON_ISSUE_ORDINAL_WORD_RE` (the 9-word ordinal denylist) and `shouldExcludeBareIssueMatch`'s ordinal-checking branch entirely — no denylist replacement, ever; red-team round 3 (`docs/reviews/qa1415fix-red-team-round3-2026-09-10.md`) demonstrated the denylist could never be complete (>= 12 residual unlisted-ordinal-word shapes measured full-tree: `items`, `rule`, `criterion`, `Next`, `recommendation`, `residual`, `Coverage gap`, `HIGH #N`, `LOW #N`, open-paren, backtick, angle-marker forms).

**Added** a positive citation-marker mechanism, replacing the negative denylist: `CITATION_MARKER_WORD_RE` (`Issue(s)`/`Closes`/`Closed`/`Fixes`/`Fixed`/`Resolves`, same-line, word-adjacent), `GH_MARKER_RE` (glued `GH`/`GH-` shorthand), `LIST_CONTINUATION_RE` (a later `#N` in the same comma/slash/"and"/`&`/dash-range list inherits an earlier marked member's status), and `classifyBareHashMatch` (replacing `shouldExcludeBareIssueMatch`). - **R3:** a bare `reponame#N` citation with no `owner/` prefix (e.g. `claude-mem#2604`) now classifies `unclassified` — never `cross-repo-issue` (asserting cross-repo for an owner-less shape was itself a directional guess) and never reaches `issueExists`/`verifyLocalIssue` as a local number.
