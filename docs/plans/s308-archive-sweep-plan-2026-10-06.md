## ADR review

- THOTH-ADR-0002 (proposed, served by the catalog): APPLICABLE. Rules the story must honor:
  - "an entry exempts a match only when its path, its patternId and the sha256 of the matched bytes all equal the entry's";
  - "Adding a value to an entry MUST arrive in a pull request whose diff shows it, with a non-empty reason; no other exemption shape (glob, line range, expiry, in-file marker, second file)";

- THOTH-ADR-0001: only relevant for its exception on the central-classification fixture, which this story does not touch. Its exception does not cover allowlist changes.
- Org ADR devops 0008: APPLICABLE (ratchet-only, no broadening). The new entry is a narrower grant for one hash at one path; ADR-0002 states this position.
