## Attacks, ranked by exposure x irreversibility x silence

### 1. K1 passes a delegated or pre-approved Human cell. BREAKS. [MED][demonstrated] (Issue #465)

The Manager asked whether the `/^Y\b/` match plus the `K1 approved` prefix is enough. The question rests on the premise that this project marks pre-approved Manager rows `N (pre-approved)`. Measured, that premise is false. The house writes `Y (pre-approved)` (8 rows, all `By = Manager`) and `N (pre-approved)` 0 times (R4). In k-readiness.ts, `isHumanY` is `/^Y\b/`. It accepts `Y (pre-approved)`, `Y (delegated: ...)`, and `Y-pending`, because the word boundary falls between `Y` and `-` (R4).

The plan defines K1 as "explicit approval recorded ... with Human = Y" (docs/plans/s308-K-wiring-plan-2026-10-05.md:122). The runbook says "the cell records the human's own words" (docs/runbooks/k-kill-switch-and-verification.md:51). A session-wide blanket pre-approval is neither.

Production trigger: a session opens with the human's blanket pre-approval, which happened on 2026-09-30 ("i preapprove your decisions/recommendations"). The Manager records "**K1 approved:** ..." in the house form `Y (pre-approved)`. It recorded a #308 activation row exactly that way on that date (decisions.md:98). Readiness then prints K1 PASS. No human ever approved K explicitly, and the row's record looks correct.

Current defense: the wiring commit is human-approved, and the human reads the readiness output. The K1 row is the only machine check that the approval was explicit, and it passes.

Exposure: 8 of 8 rows this house wrote under a session pre-approval carry `Y (pre-approved...)`, and every such row with a `K1 approved` prefix would read PASS. Basis: counted (R4). It fails silently and the PASS line looks correct, but it is reversible: K can be unwired with the kill switch.

**Answer to the Manager's question: not sufficient.** A cell that contains "pre-approved" or "delegated", or anything other than a bare `Y` or the human's own words, must FAIL.

Required (one failing test): `k-readiness: K1 fails on a delegated, pre-approved or hyphen-suffixed Human cell`. Suggested fix, the implementer's call: accept only a bare `Y` or the form `Y (human, <date>: "<words>")`, and reject any cell containing pre-approved or delegated. K4 shares `isHumanY`; decide whether K4 needs the same tightening.
